Optimal Clusters Selection

The Elbow Method displays the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (WCSS) against the number of clusters. The "elbow" point is where the rate of decrease shifts from rapid to slow, indicating the optimal balance between cluster compactness and count.

A sharper bend or elbow suggest the most natural groupings in your customer dataset.

Clustering Quality Validation

The Silhouette Score measures how similar a customer profile is to its own cluster compared to other clusters. Scores range from -1 to +1.

  • Score near +1 Indicates customers are perfectly matched to their assigned cluster.
  • Score near 0 Indicates overlapping clusters or profiles sitting on boundaries.
  • Score near -1 Indicates customers are misassigned.

We target the number of clusters that maximizes the silhouette score.
